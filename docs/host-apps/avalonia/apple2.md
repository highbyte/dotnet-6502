# Apple II Plus in the Avalonia apps

Apple II Plus-specific features in the [Avalonia Browser](browser.md) and
[Avalonia Desktop](desktop.md) apps. The two runtimes share almost all of this;
differences are called out inline.

For the emulated system's capabilities and current limitations, see the
[Apple II system overview](../../systems/apple2/overview.md).
